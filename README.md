# My Favorite Movie Series: Fast & Furious

![Fast and Furious](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/b/b8/The_Fast_and_the_Furious_poster.jpg)

## About the Series
**Fast & Furious** is an action franchise about street racing, heists and *family*. It began in 2001 with *The Fast and the Furious* and grew from illegal street races into global missions.

> Family is the heart of the whole series.

---

## Main Characters
| Character | Actor | Role in the story |
|-----------|-------|-------------------|
| Dominic Toretto | Vin Diesel | Leader of the crew |
| Brian O'Conner | Paul Walker | Undercover cop who joins the family |
| Letty Ortiz | Michelle Rodriguez | Dom's partner and a skilled racer |
| Roman Pearce | Tyrese Gibson | Brian's loyal friend |
| Tej Parker | Ludacris | The crew's tech expert |

## Why I Like It
- Exciting car chases and stunts
- Strong themes of **loyalty** and **teamwork**
- Fun characters that you grow attached to
- Every movie tops the last one in action

## Movies I Recommend
1. *The Fast and the Furious* (2001)
2. *Fast Five* (2011)
3. *Furious 7* (2015)
